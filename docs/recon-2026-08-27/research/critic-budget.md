## Confirmed

1. **Compute kernel is at 8/8 storage buffers.** `src/webgl/gpgpu/gpgpuNodeSim.ts:1296-1304` (`positionBuffer, velocityBuffer, homeA..D`), `:1426` (`startBuffer`), `:1444` (`delayBuffer`); all eight are `.element(instanceIndex)`-read/written inside `simulate` (`:1486-1491, 1502, 1507`). Default WebGPU `maxStorageBuffersPerShaderStage` = 8 and the renderer requests no `requiredLimits` (`src/webgl/renderer/createRenderer.ts:96-111` passes only `antialias/powerPreference/forceWebGL`; `WebGPUBackend.js:71` defaults `requiredLimits = {}`). "A 5th person = kernel re-architecture" is correct.
2. **Vertex stage = 4 vertex buffers + 4 storage bindings.** Vertex buffers: quad `position` (`:1584`), `positionBuffer.toAttribute()` (`:2020`, also `:1781`, `:1881` — same `bufferAttribute`, same slot since `WebGPUAttributeUtils` keys by attribute), `velocityBuffer.toAttribute()` (`:1620`), `delayBuffer.toAttribute()` (`:1634`). The lit path adds **only uniforms** (`:1803-1846`), so "zero new bindings" is true.
3. **`[rgb24, nx, ny, ink]` packing is exact.** `packTint` `:1361-1386` encodes 8-bit sRGB as `r·65536 + g·256 + b ≤ 16,777,215 < 2^24` — exactly representable in f32; decode `:1850-1858` uses `floor/div` on that integer. `"vec4"` storage is unpadded (`WebGPUAttributeUtils.js:105-116` rewrites only `itemSize === 3`), so `.element(i).w` is the real ink.
4. **Varying discipline (HANDOFF contract 3) is respected in the lit path.** All lit terms are self-contained expressions (`litBlurExpr :1893`, `litLightExpr :1920`, `portraitColorExpr :2170`) fed to `varying()` at `:2107-2120, 2213`; none is an outer `.toVar()` assigned inside the vertex `Fn`. `litLightExpr` uses `litModelPos = positionBuffer.toAttribute().xyz` (`:1881`) — the correct swizzle for a padded `"vec3"` `.toAttribute()` (contract 8).
5. **`.toAttribute()` vs `.element()` width contract is real** — `WebGPUAttributeUtils.js:105-116` mutates `itemSize 3 → 4` for storage attributes; `WGSLNodeBuilder.js:1037-1049` (`getNodeAccess`) returns `READ_ONLY` outside compute and emits `var<storage, read>`. Fix the HANDOFF line, not the store.
7. **B3.1's kernel pre-divide is geometrically incomplete and would still shear.** The group sits OFF the camera axis (`FounderPortraitMorph.tsx:1163-1167`: `(cx − vw/2)·k, (ih/2 − cy)·k`, then rotated by sway/parallax). Projection is `(X_g + x)/(D − z)`; pinning a cell to its lattice position needs `x' = x·(D−z)/D − X_g·z/D`. The proposed `target.xy *= (D − z)/D` omits the `−X_g·z/D` term (a per-cell lateral shift ∝ group screen offset × relief), and the rest sway/parallax rotation is applied *after* the kernel, so the pre-divided shell is sheared under any yaw (the synthesis half-admits this and proposes damping the parallax). Do it in the **vertex stage** instead (A2): zero bindings, exact for any group offset, no shear.
8. **B6's rim term `smoothstep(0.03, 0, |n.z|)` will practically never fire.** Normals are rebuilt as `z = sqrt(max(1 − x² − y², 0))` (`:1861-1866`) from central differences of a 3×3-blurred 8-bit depth (`sampleImagePoints.ts:447-496, 536-549`), so `n.z` is ≥ 0 by construction and the fraction of cells with `n.z < 0.03` is a sliver (only at near-vertical depth ramps that survived the blur). Lusion's data is a true scan with 88 back-facing points and a genuine silhouette; SerSan's is a height field. See A9.
9. **B2's count estimate is off.** `150×210` = 31,500 cells; with the measured lit presence fraction 46.5k / 117,450 = 39.6 % the union is ≈ 12.5k, not 18-22k (the synthesis scaled with the legacy 44 %). See A7.

## Amendments

- **A1 — B2 pixel floor: apply AFTER the perspective divide and keep the coverage varying in sync.** In `sizeNode` (`:2083-2095`) the on-screen diameter is `S/dist` (the `·clip.w` at `:2100` cancels the divide: NDC offset = `corner·(S/dist)·2/viewport`). The floor is therefore `sizePx = max(S/dist, uMinPx)` and the energy term `pow((S/dist)/sizePx, 1.5)` must multiply `portraitColorExpr` (vertex stage, `:2170`), never the fragment (zero-binding path). `portraitSizePxExpr` (`:1766-1773`) omits `/dist` and is divided in the fragment (`:2282-2287`); with a floor it becomes `max(S, uMinPx·dist)`, or simply drop `cov²` on the lit path — with `uMinPx ≥ PORTRAIT_COV_MIN_PX` the coverage term is provably `≡ 1`. Also raise the `defPointSize` clamp `[10, 96]` (`:843-847`): at 15k particles / dpr 1 / a 26 rem (416 px) stage the 2.4× regime needs ≈ 99 and is clipped today.
- **A2 — Replace B3.1 with a vertex-stage "flattened divide" (zero bindings, exact).** After `mv = modelViewMatrix·vec4(p,1)` (`:2060`): `clipFlat = cameraProjectionMatrix·vec4(mv.xy, −uViewDist, 1)`, `clip = mix(clip, clipFlat, uPrediv)`; keep the true `mv.z` for `dist`, DoF and lighting. `uPrediv = 1 − env` is written next to `uRelief` (`FounderPortraitMorph.tsx:1233`); `uViewDist = CAMERA_Z − dolly` already exists as `uFocusDist` (`:1231`). Exact for any group offset and rotation; the kernel stays untouched (no risk to contract 10's kernel↔varying lockstep). Keep B3.2 (silhouette dilation in `reliefField`) — sampler-only, harmless.
- **A3 — B4: decide DPR at the renderer or not at all.** If retina fill matters, change `createRenderer.ts:148-152` (site-wide; hero, spores, neural and crystal islands inherit it) and re-QA them; otherwise drop B4. Never let `dprNow` diverge from `gl.getPixelRatio()`.
- **A4 — Rewrite the §4 invariants and §5.2 to the tree:** no scroll gate; prev/next buttons + arrow keys; `pinned` = DOM morph mode; `founders-rail.tsx` already rewritten. QA keeps `simulateGesture('down'|'up')` (= next/prev).
- **A5 — §5.1 step 7:** "extend `HANDOFF:297-329`" (contracts 13-16 exist): add the B2 regime numbers, the licence rule, the DPR decision, and correct "soglia 0.8" → 1.0.
- **A6 — B5 (baked AO) as proposed runs on the main thread at every rebuild.** `samplePortraitSet` runs in-browser (`FounderPortraitMorph.tsx:617, 980`) and the build effect re-runs on `measureVersion`/`size.width` (`:1021`). A horizon AO over the 117k-cell field (≈5.6 M ops × 4 portraits, with `atan`) is ≈ 100-300 ms of jank per resize/measure. Bake AO **offline into the twin** (`generate-founder-depth.mjs` writes R = depth, G = AO; `readGrid` already decodes the twin) — this also matches Lusion's pre-baked `nShade.w` — or cache per `sampleEpoch`.
- **A7 — B2 grid target:** for 15-25k particles at the measured 39.6 % presence fraction use ≈ 175×245 (42,875 cells → ≈ 17k) or 200×280 (56,000 → ≈ 22k), then verify with `__sersanFounderMorph.getSampler()`. `MAX_COUNT_BY_TIER.full 80000` can stay; after B2 the binding constraint is the `defPointSize` clamp (A1), not the stride cliff.
- **A8 — B6 rim:** gate on the presence edge instead of `|n.z|`: the ±`depthEdge` band (`ink` in 0.05-0.95 before the fade, `sampleImagePoints.ts:420-427`) is the true silhouette in this representation; or use `smoothstep(0.25, 0.05, n.z)` on the height-field normal. Keep it under the 1.0 cap except on the scanline pass so only the rim crosses into bloom (threshold 1.0).
- **A9 — B7 (luminance-detail normals)** is safe budget-wise (sampler-only) but must respect contract 2: the detail term must be masked by presence, or the white-wall/skin luminance gradient at the silhouette re-enters the normal field.
