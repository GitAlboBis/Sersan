### W1 · TEXT v3 — "the real Lusion" (kills the fill-wipe)

Files: `problem-section.tsx`, `production-grade-section.tsx`, `use-type-scrub.ts` (rewrite or
replace with a new `fx/lusion-type.ts`), possibly `roll-letters.tsx` (align to R1 exactly).

The dossier's key finding: Lusion's feel = **(a) viewport-entry choreography that REPLAYS**
(reset off-screen, replay on re-entry) **+ (b) continuous per-frame parallax drift** of text
blocks by scroll offset (differential k per block) — NOT a scroll-scrubbed fill. Port with
GSAP (`CustomEase.create("lusion","0.35,0,0,1")` once, site-wide):
1. **Display words** (causes "No evals…", effect ghost words, production kickers): recipe
   **R1 letter-roll** exactly — per-char column of 4 stacked copies in a 1em overflow mask,
   y −500%→0, `expo.inOut`, ~1.25s, **center-out cosine stagger** (GSAP
   `stagger:{each:0.06, from:"center"}`). Ghost words KEEP the text-stroke ghost style at
   rest and the amber/ink hover ignition — but their ENTRANCE is the roll, no fill-wipe.
2. **Production claims** (full sentences): recipe **H3** — words in line masks, y 100%→0
   (1s, lusion ease, 0.025s/word) + x 200px→0 delayed +0.4s. Chapter h2s: same H3 or H2
   char-roll (implementer's taste; H2 for the shorter problem title lines).
3. **Eyebrows/callouts**: keep the existing scramble (it matches S1 — verify head=5,
   40 chars/s, charset 33–125; label variant head=2 @15fps).
4. **REPLAY**: every animation resets to FROM state when its section fully leaves the
   viewport and replays on re-entry (Lusion `_needsReset`). IO-driven, once:false + an
   explicit off-screen reset. RM/SSR/no-JS: settled visible, never hidden (site discipline).
5. **Per-frame drift** (the scroll-bound life): each text block gets Lusion's
   `showScreenOffset` parallax — translateY = −k·(distance of block from viewport center),
   k ≈ 0.5 title / 1.5 body / 1.25 secondary, recomputed per frame from Lenis scroll (ONE
   rAF-coalesced writer per section, transform-only, no gBCR in the loop — cache rects on
   measure). This replaces the fill-wipe as the "animate with scroll" answer.
6. **Hover**: keep river ignition; adopt Hv1 exactly on rows with arrows (chars slide
   0→1.5em with the right-to-left wave, arrow slides into the vacated space).
